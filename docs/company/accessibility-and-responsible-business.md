# Accessibility and responsible-business statement

**Status:** product/legal/people review required
**Owner:** `[PRODUCT/PEOPLE OWNER]`

## 1. Accessibility

The company should document the target accessibility standard, such as WCAG
2.1 or 2.2 AA, for the applicable web console, reports, and customer-facing
materials. The statement must be based on testing, not intention.

Record:

- product/version and tested routes;
- keyboard navigation;
- focus order and visible focus;
- screen-reader behavior;
- headings, labels, forms, tables, and errors;
- color contrast and non-color cues;
- zoom/reflow and responsive behavior;
- captions/transcripts for media;
- PDF/report accessibility;
- known exceptions;
- remediation owner and date; and
- request contact for an accommodation.

Do not claim full WCAG conformance without an appropriate assessment and scope.

## 2. Responsible business

Maintain approved policies for:

- anti-bribery and corruption;
- conflicts of interest;
- whistleblowing and non-retaliation;
- non-discrimination and respectful workplace;
- modern slavery and forced labor;
- child labor prohibition;
- responsible sourcing;
- sanctions and export controls;
- environmental and security responsibilities; and
- supplier code of conduct.

## 3. Customer response

Until the actual assessment is complete, use:

> The company is preparing a deployment- and entity-specific accessibility and
> responsible-business statement. Applicable policies, assessment scope,
> exceptions, owner, and review date will be supplied through the procurement
> process. No universal conformance claim is made by this template.
