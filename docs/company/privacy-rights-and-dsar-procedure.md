# Privacy rights and data-subject request procedure

**Status:** privacy operations template
**Owner:** `[PRIVACY OWNER/DPO]`
**Contact:** `[PRIVACY REQUEST CONTACT]`

## 1. Intake

Accept requests through `[CHANNELS]`. Record receipt date, requestor,
customer/controller, identity-verification method, jurisdiction, requested
right, systems, deadline, and assigned owner.

Route requests concerning Customer Data to the applicable customer unless the
customer instructs Vendor to respond as required by law.

## 2. Rights workflow

1. Authenticate requestor to a level proportionate to risk.
2. Identify controller, customer, environment, and data scope.
3. Determine applicable law and deadline.
4. Search active service records and approved exports.
5. Apply legal hold or incident preservation where needed.
6. Consult Customer for Customer-controlled data.
7. Redact third-party, privileged, secret, or legally exempt data.
8. Complete access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability,
   or consent-withdrawal action.
9. Record exceptions and reason.
10. Obtain privacy review.
11. Respond securely and record date.
12. Verify downstream and backup handling.
13. Close and retain the request record.

## 4. Security and retention

Do not send Personal Data through unapproved email or expose one person's data
to another customer. Retain the request record under the approved retention
schedule, then delete it when its legal, support, and audit purpose expires.
