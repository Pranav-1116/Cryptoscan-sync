# AI and customer-data use statement

**Status:** product/legal/privacy approval required
**Purpose:** answer customer questions about AI, machine learning, models,
training, automation, and Customer Data

## 1. Product position

CryptoScan's primary analysis path is deterministic and policy-driven. Unknown
and not-evaluated results are not silently converted into PASS. Any feature
that uses statistical, machine-learning, generative, or external model
behavior must be identified in the product and reviewed under this statement.

## 2. Customer Data use

For the contracted Services, Customer Data may be processed to:

- collect and analyze approved sources;
- generate inventory, findings, relationships, reports, and evidence;
- secure, troubleshoot, support, monitor, and recover the service;
- prevent abuse and investigate incidents; and
- improve service reliability where the DPA and contract permit.

Customer Data must not be used to train a general-purpose model, sold, or used
for unrelated advertising without explicit written customer authorization,
lawful basis, and updated privacy terms.

## 3. External model/provider use

Before any external model or provider receives Customer Data, record:

- provider and legal entity;
- model/version;
- exact data fields and prompts;
- region and transfer mechanism;
- retention and training settings;
- security and subprocessor status;
- human review and output limitations;
- customer notice/approval; and
- deletion and audit method.

Do not state `no AI`, `no model`, or `no external processing` until the actual
release and subprocessor architecture has been reviewed.

## 4. Output limitations

Automated or model-assisted output may be incomplete, wrong, biased, stale, or
ambiguous. A human owner must review material findings before production,
employment, regulatory, legal, or risk decisions. Record model/version,
input scope, reviewer, and limitation when the feature is material.

## 5. Prohibited use

The Service must not be used to:

- make an unreviewed decision about a person;
- infer sensitive personal traits without a lawful approved purpose;
- upload credentials, private keys, or unnecessary Personal Data to a model;
- bypass customer authorization or data classification;
- represent generated text as an auditor, regulator, or customer statement; or
- hide `UNKNOWN`, `SKIPPED`, `FAIL`, or `NOT_EVALUATED` states.

## 6. Customer answer

Until a release-specific AI/data-use review is approved, answer:

> CryptoScan's core product path is deterministic and policy-driven. The
> customer deployment and release must be reviewed for any model-assisted or
> external-model feature. Customer Data is not used for general-purpose model
> training unless expressly authorized in the DPA or Order Form. Material
> automated output remains subject to human review and documented limitations.
