# Personnel and physical security evidence guide

**Status:** HR, security, legal, and provider evidence guide
**Owner:** `[PEOPLE/SECURITY OWNER]`

## 1. Personnel controls

Document the actual policy and evidence for:

- pre-employment screening where lawful and proportionate;
- confidentiality and acceptable-use commitments;
- role-specific security and privacy training;
- secure development and privileged-access training;
- conflict-of-interest and ethics acknowledgement;
- joiner, mover, and leaver access removal;
- disciplinary and policy-violation process;
- contractor and temporary-staff controls;
- remote-work and device requirements; and
- annual training/review records.

Do not provide raw HR records to a customer. Provide policy, scope, completion
rate, attestation, or independent provider evidence through the approved data
room.

## 2. Privileged personnel

For privileged access, record:

- role and business need;
- manager/security approval;
- identity and MFA;
- time-bound access;
- PAM or equivalent control;
- activity logging;
- periodic review;
- break-glass review; and
- termination confirmation.

## 3. Physical security

For Vendor-controlled hosting or offices, document:

- data-centre provider and region;
- physical access control;
- visitor management;
- CCTV and alarm controls;
- environmental protection;
- power and connectivity resilience;
- media storage and destruction;
- clean desk/device policy;
- office access and remote support; and
- provider assurance report.

For cloud infrastructure, identify the provider's responsibility and applicable
independent evidence. Do not claim Vendor controls a provider facility unless
it actually does.

## 4. Evidence response

Use policy excerpts, provider reports, training attestations, access-review
records, and a responsibility matrix. Redact names, background checks,
security layouts, and personal information not necessary for the question.
