# Company documentation completeness review and closure plan

**Review date:** 2026-09-29
**Scope:** legal, privacy, security assurance, procurement, operational
resilience, and vendor-questionnaire readiness
**Status:** planning register; not a certification or legal opinion

The company pack now contains the primary contract templates, privacy terms,
security addendum, assurance guide, architecture templates, retention,
subprocessor, and questionnaire-answer materials. The most important remaining
documents are the execution, evidence, and operating records that turn those
templates into a customer-ready package.

## 2. What is not solved by writing another document

The following require external action rather than more prose:

- SOC 2 examination and report;
- ISO/IEC 27001 certification;
- independent penetration testing and retest;
- actual HSM/KMS provider qualification;
- customer pilot accuracy validation;
- customer-approved production topology;
- customer/regulator risk acceptance;
- real support staffing and on-call rota;
- actual residency and transfer approval;
- customer-specific DPA/SLA/MSA signatures;
- insurance certificates;
- recurring access, patch, incident, backup, and DR records.

A document can define the evidence required, but it cannot impersonate the
issuer, customer, auditor, regulator, or production operator.

## 3. Recommended order of completion

### Phase 1 — contract and identity foundation

1. Confirm legal entity, registration, tax, contracting address, signatory, and
   governing law.
2. Complete MSA, NDA, DPA, SLA, Security Addendum, and Order Form.
3. Create the pilot/implementation SOW and responsibility matrix.
4. Populate the actual security contact, privacy contact, support contact, and
   escalation route.
5. Approve the subprocessor and residency register.

### Phase 2 — customer security package

1. Complete customer-specific security architecture and data flow.
2. Complete encryption/key/HSM architecture.
3. Complete backup/DR and continuity plan.
4. Create the evidence data room and release procedure.
5. Complete questionnaire answer-bank review and answer expiry.
6. Attach actual release, SBOM, vulnerability, and operational evidence.

### Phase 3 — privacy and operations

1. Create records of processing and DPIA/transfer assessments.
2. Test DSAR, deletion, export, and legal-hold processes.
3. Approve retention schedules and subprocessor notices.
4. Publish vulnerability disclosure and incident contacts.
5. Approve support handbook, escalation, and on-call coverage.

### Phase 4 — external assurance and recurring evidence

1. Select independent penetration tester.
2. Decide SOC 2/ISO scope and audit plan.
3. Qualify actual HSM/KMS provider.
4. Run topology-realistic DR, restore, load, and support exercises.
5. Collect signed customer pilot validation and residual-risk approval.
6. Refresh the assurance pack on the defined review cadence.

## 4. Completion rule

The pack is customer-ready only when every document has:

- legal entity and service scope;
- version and effective date;
- owner and independent reviewer;
- approval authority and approval date;
- applicable jurisdiction and deployment model;
- evidence references and confidentiality classification;
- expiry or next-review date;
- exception and change process; and
- a clear statement of what the document does not prove.

The company should not answer `complete` merely because a template exists.
