# Records of processing activities template

**Status:** privacy governance template
**Owner:** `[PRIVACY OWNER/DPO]`
**Review:** at least annually and after material processing changes

Maintain one record per processing activity. This is not a generic statement
that all processing is lawful; it is an inventory used to assess lawful basis,
risk, transfers, retention, and controls.

## 2. Required detail per activity

For each row record:

- lawful basis and transparency notice;
- special-category or sensitive data;
- system and storage region;
- transfer and subprocessor;
- retention and deletion trigger;
- access roles and review;
- security measures;
- rights-request process;
- DPIA/transfer-assessment reference;
- incident contact;
- owner, reviewer, effective date, and next review.

## 3. Change triggers

Create or update a record when the company adds:

- a new source or data category;
- a new purpose or analytics use;
- a new region or subprocessor;
- a new support or remote-access route;
- a new retention or deletion behavior;
- automated decision or profiling;
- a new product feature involving Personal Data; or
- a new legal entity or customer role.
