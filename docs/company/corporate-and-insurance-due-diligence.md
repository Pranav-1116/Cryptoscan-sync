# Corporate, insurance, and supplier due-diligence pack

**Status:** finance/legal/procurement template
**Owner:** `[FINANCE/LEGAL OWNER]`

Banks commonly request corporate identity, financial viability, insurance,
ownership, litigation, sanctions, anti-bribery, and supplier-responsibility
information in addition to technical security answers.

## 1. Corporate identity

Prepare through an approved secure channel:

- legal entity name and registration number;
- incorporation jurisdiction and registered address;
- contracting and invoicing entity;
- ownership/control information where required;
- tax registration and invoice details;
- authorized signatory evidence;
- parent/affiliate relationship;
- business contact and service address; and
- relevant licences or registrations.

Do not put identity documents or sensitive financial records in the public
repository.

## 3. Financial and continuity information

The customer may request:

- audited or management financial statements;
- going-concern and funding information;
- continuity and succession plan;
- key-person dependency;
- material litigation or regulatory action;
- bankruptcy/insolvency information;
- disaster recovery and supplier continuity;
- critical subprocessor dependency; and
- exit/transition plan.

Disclose only through the approved procurement data room and under
confidentiality terms.

## 4. Ethical and regulatory declarations

Complete actual approved policies or declarations for:

- anti-bribery and corruption;
- sanctions and export controls;
- conflicts of interest;
- gifts and hospitality;
- whistleblowing and non-retaliation;
- forced labor, child labor, and modern slavery;
- human rights and responsible sourcing;
- environmental responsibilities where requested;
- fair competition;
- tax compliance; and
- supplier code of conduct.

A template or internal intention is not a signed supplier declaration.
