# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement template

**Template status:** counsel review required; not an executed agreement
**Version:** `NDA-DRAFT-1.0`
**Parties:** `[VENDOR LEGAL NAME]` and `[CUSTOMER LEGAL NAME]`
**Effective date:** `[EFFECTIVE DATE]`

This template is for discovery, technical evaluation, pilot planning,
security review, procurement, and commercial negotiation. Use a one-way form
when only one party will disclose information, or use the mutual form below
when both parties will disclose.

## 1. Purpose

The parties wish to evaluate a potential business relationship involving
CryptoScan, including product demonstrations, technical information, pilot
scope, security architecture, customer estate information, pricing, and
contract negotiation (the **Purpose**).

Neither party is required to disclose information or enter another agreement.
The NDA does not grant a licence, require a purchase, or authorize scanning,
access, or processing of Customer systems.

## 2. Confidential Information

**Confidential Information** means non-public information disclosed by or on
behalf of a party that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person
would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of
disclosure. It includes:

- business plans, pricing, forecasts, customers, suppliers, and negotiations;
- software, source code, architecture, security controls, vulnerabilities,
  credentials, and non-public product roadmaps;
- customer systems, repositories, configurations, certificates, keys,
  inventories, logs, network diagrams, and risk findings;
- Personal Data and regulated or commercially sensitive information; and
- the existence and content of discussions where the parties agree that it is
  sensitive.

Oral disclosures should be identified as confidential when made and summarized
in writing within `[30]` days where practical.

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information the receiving party can
demonstrate:

1. was public without breach;
2. was already lawfully known without a duty of confidentiality;
3. was received lawfully from a third party without restriction;
4. was independently developed without using the discloser's information; or
5. was approved for release in writing by the discloser.

The exclusions do not remove obligations for Personal Data, trade secrets,
security credentials, or information protected by another applicable law.

## 4. Use and protection

The receiving party will:

- use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
- disclose it only to personnel, professional advisers, affiliates, and
  approved contractors who need it and are bound by confidentiality duties;
- protect it using at least the care used for its own similar information and
  no less than reasonable care;
- not reverse engineer, misuse, scan, probe, or access systems except under a
  separate written authorization;
- not remove markings or defeat access controls; and
- promptly notify the discloser of suspected unauthorized use or disclosure.

Each party is responsible for its recipients' compliance.

## 5. Compelled disclosure

A receiving party may disclose Confidential Information when required by law,
subpoena, court order, regulator, or stock-exchange rule. To the extent legally
permitted, it will provide advance notice, disclose only the required portion,
and reasonably cooperate with protective measures. Disclosure to a regulator
or auditor for a lawful review is not a breach when limited to the requirement.

## 6. Security and Personal Data

The parties will not disclose private keys, passwords, payment secrets, or
production credentials under this NDA. Personal Data must be disclosed only
under an approved lawful basis and, where applicable, a DPA. The NDA does not
replace the DPA, Security Addendum, or data-processing instructions.

The parties will use secure transfer methods approved for the information
classification and will not place sensitive information in public repositories,
unencrypted email, or unapproved collaboration systems.

## 7. Return and destruction

On request or when the Purpose ends, the receiving party will return or destroy
Confidential Information, subject to:

- legal or regulatory retention;
- automatic backup copies that are isolated, access-restricted, and deleted
  under the ordinary retention cycle; and
- one archival copy retained by legal or compliance solely to prove obligations.

The receiving party will confirm destruction on request, except where a
technical or legal limitation is documented.

## 8. Ownership and no licence

All Confidential Information remains the discloser's property. Disclosure
creates no licence, assignment, warranty, or obligation to proceed. Any IP
rights in product feedback, work product, or evaluation materials must be
addressed in the MSA or Order Form.

## 9. Term and survival

This NDA begins on the Effective Date and continues for `[3]` years unless
terminated earlier by written notice. Confidentiality obligations continue for
`[5]` years after disclosure, and trade secrets remain protected for as long
as they qualify as trade secrets under applicable law. Personal Data and
regulated information remain protected under applicable law and the DPA.

## 10. Remedies

Unauthorized use or disclosure may cause irreparable harm. The discloser may
seek injunctive or equitable relief where available, in addition to other
remedies. This clause does not waive proof or any mandatory legal limitation.

## 11. General

Complete governing law, venue, notices, assignment, waiver, severability,
relationship, export controls, and entire-agreement provisions with counsel.
The parties may execute electronically and in counterparts.
