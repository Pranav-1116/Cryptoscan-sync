## 2. Website data

Record:

- server logs and IP handling;
- browser/device and diagnostic data;
- forms and support/contact data;
- analytics provider and settings;
- authentication/session data;
- embedded content and third-party scripts;
- consent-management platform;
- marketing and opt-out mechanism; and
- international transfer and retention.

## 3. Choice and withdrawal

Visitors must be able to:

- accept or reject non-essential cookies;
- change preferences later;
- see the purpose and provider;
- obtain necessary service without being forced into non-essential tracking;
- withdraw consent as easily as it was given; and
- access the updated notice.

## 4. Review evidence

Before publishing, retain:

- technology scan of the actual website;
- cookie register;
- consent-banner configuration;
- privacy/legal approval;
- region/provider review; and
- change record after deployment.
